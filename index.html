      Questions clinics ask first

      More answers on the support site, or email support@vetcove.com.

      Will Vetcove work with my suppliers?

      Almost certainly. Vetcove supports nearly every major veterinary vendor in the U.S., including distributors, manufacturers, diagnostic labs, compounding pharmacies and office supply companies. You'll see exactly which accounts you can add as soon as you sign up.

      Will I still get my negotiated, GPO or corporate pricing?

      Yes. Buying group deals, corporate group pricing and anything your reps have negotiated show up automatically once you connect that vendor. You still qualify for all rebates, promotions and loyalty programs.

      Is Vetcove a distributor or a buying group?

      Neither. Vetcove doesn't buy and resell products, and doesn't negotiate deals on your behalf. It's a purchasing platform that helps you order from the suppliers you already use, with a few Vetcove-exclusive deals on top.

      Do I have to change who I order from?

      No. You keep the same vendors, pricing, shipping, billing and support. Your reps still receive their commissions. The only thing that changes is how ordering feels.

      Why do I need to add my vendor accounts?

      Suppliers charge each clinic different prices for the same item, depending on your GPO, corporate group or the tier you've negotiated. Connecting your accounts lets Vetcove show your clinic's own prices and local stock, and place orders with your vendors for you.
